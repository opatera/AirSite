Your landing page is no longer just a placeholder. It now works as a launcher for internal tools, with room to keep adding services over time.

The first tool is AirServer, built for fast drag-and-drop uploads into your shared storage from any browser on the network.

What AirServer does
  • Lets users browse from `/share` downward without exposing directories above it.
  • Supports drag-and-drop or picker-based uploads.
  • Refreshes the folder view after uploads complete.
